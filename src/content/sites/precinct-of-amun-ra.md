## Getting there & what to know

**Location:** East Bank, Luxor, ~3km from Luxor Temple.

**Hours:** ~6:00am–5:30pm winter / 6:00am–6:00pm summer — confirm before publish.

**Entrance fee:** reported ~450–600 EGP for foreign adults (2024–2026 range), covers the whole Karnak complex including Mut and Khonsu/Opet — confirm current price at publish.

**Recommended time:** 1.5–2 hrs for this precinct alone.

**Sound & Light Show:** separate ticket, ~300 EGP, three nightly showings.

**Tips:** shade only in the Hypostyle Hall; water and a hat non-negotiable; tripods/video need a separate permit.
